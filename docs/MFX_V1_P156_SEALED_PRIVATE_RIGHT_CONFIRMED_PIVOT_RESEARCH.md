For a candidate high pivot, its HIGH is strictly above every LEFT and RIGHT bar high. For a candidate low pivot, LOW is strictly below every LEFT and RIGHT low. Equal neighbor values invalidate the candidate; a single unusually wide candle satisfying both extrema is ambiguous and discarded. Right confirmation uses the source open label of the LAST required RIGHT candle + frame width, and requires that candle to be **CLOSED at the asof decision clock**. Never use the current open bar, future candle, noncontiguous history, unfinished derived timeframe or original source with wrong SHA. The formula records a private *candle-close timestamp*, NOT actual publisher first-known/retrieval timestamp, vendor signature, observation rights or historical replay custody.

A present timeframe with fewer than `left+right+1` consecutive closed candles is `INSUFFICIENT_CONTIGUOUS_CLOSED_BARS`; the research never silently hallucinates H4/D1 history. **D1 is deliberately not scanned** even when many days are available: native source uses Australia/Sydney local-session dates with independently unqualified session gap/holiday/DST continuity and owner-HOLD native 2026-11-11 numeric source revision.
